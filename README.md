# PANGANOVA

### Food Price & Economic Intelligence Platform

**Understand Food Prices. Discover Economic Insights.**

PANGANOVA is a national economic intelligence platform designed to monitor food price movements, compare commodity trends, and explore household consumption indicators in Indonesia.

## Project Overview

PANGANOVA transforms food price and economic datasets into meaningful analytical visualizations to support better understanding of Indonesia's food market dynamics.

## Core Features

- Executive Summary
- Price Trend Exploration
- Commodity Comparison
- Food Prices & Purchasing Power
- Insights & Methodology

## Project Status

Currently under development.

Initial development focuses on three analytical modules:

1. Executive Summary
2. Price Trend Exploration
